General Safety Rules

  • Read the manual completely before use.
  • Only connect to mains matching the voltage on the type badge.
  • Never store or use near heat or gas sources.
  • Disconnect power before cleaning or maintenance.
  • Protect from splash water — never pull the cable to unplug.
  • Never put the machine or cable into water.
  • Water hardness max 8°dH — otherwise a filter is mandatory.
  • Place on stable, firm ground, out of reach of small children.
  • If machine or cable is damaged → take out of service, contact service centre.
  • Never use outdoors. Protect from weather.
  • Only original spare parts.
Danger — Electric shock Always turn off the machine and pull the plug before performing maintenance or repair work.
Caution — Scalding Hot surfaces and hot water inside. Allow the machine to cool before cleaning. After selecting Fill Boiler or Empty Boiler the machine immediately dispenses hot water — place a vessel first.
Caution — Crushing The fresh brewing unit and espresso brewing unit have parts that move on their own. Never put hands inside while the machine is switched on.
Caution — Pressure Before working on the boiler or pipes: interrupt the water supply → dispense hot water until Check inlet appears → disconnect from mains.
Attention — Destruction Never hose the machine down or use a high-pressure cleaner. Risk of electric shock and internal damage.
Hygiene Wash hands before replenishing. Avoid direct contact with product. Do not cough or sneeze into containers. Do not touch the inside of a container after cleaning. Respect use-by dates.

Wear Parts (not covered by warranty)

Mechanical parts and parts in the hot-water circuit: hoses, seals, outlets and similar. Defects caused by wear must be resolved by manufacturer-trained service technicians.

Installation & First Setup

Machine may only be set up and started by trained personnel.

Steps for First Use

1. Unpack→ 2. Position→ 3. Water supply→ 4. Mains electricity→ 5. Emergency lock→ 6. Fill products→ 7. Fill cups→ 8. Fill sticks (ES)→ 9. Switch on→ 10. Fill boiler→ 11. Programme→ 12. Calibrate + final control

Positioning

Important Dry, well-ventilated room only. Even ground. Align with a spirit level using the four adjustable feet.
  • Back clearance: min. 60 mm (ventilation + water supply).
  • Right clearance: 5 mm (door opening angle > 90°).
  • Water hose must not be bent.
  • Enough room around machine for maintenance, cleaning and repair.

Water Connection

  1. Only use mains with drinking-safe water. Rinse the mains thoroughly before connecting.
  2. Lead the water hose through the opening at the back.
  3. Connect to the connection spout (G3/8" outer thread) inside the machine.
  4. Make sure the connection is tight.
Note Allowed water pressure 2–5 bar. Lower → separate water pump required.
Water hardness max 8°dH — otherwise a water filter must be used.

Electrical Connection

  • Check the type badge for correct voltage/frequency.
  • Do not connect to a network subject to voltage variations from heavy-duty machinery.
  • Use a grounded socket with protective grounding.
  • The plug must remain easily accessible so power can be interrupted.

Emergency Lock Installation

  1. Remove the faceplate at the front (pull out in arrow direction).
  2. Insert a screwdriver into the door opening and push the bolting bar upwards — door is unbolted.
  3. Take the emergency lock out of the grounds bucket.
  4. Put it into the opening at the machine door.
  5. Turn until it engages in its final position, then pull out the key.

Opening the Door with an IR Key

Hold the IR key towards the machine door and press the send button. The door is unbolted.

Note When using the technician's key, the belonging password must be keyed in additionally. The IR key must be ordered separately.

Coffee Grinder Setting (BTC / ES)

Note Default setting is for reference beans. Grinding level is changed with the screw in the grinder lid. Turning direction grob/fein is depicted on the lid. Only trained personnel may change the grinding level.
  1. Make sure there are no coffee beans in the grinder.
  2. Loosen the counter disk with a face spanner for angle grinders (35 mm).
  3. Activate the grinder in the operator menu.
  4. With the grinder running, turn the adjustment screw clockwise (fein) until the grinding wheels touch.
  5. With the grinder running, turn counter-clockwise (grob) to the desired level (1 line = 28 µm).
  6. Filter level → open 17 lines. Espresso level → open 9 lines.
  7. Switch the grinder off.
  8. Pull the counter disk tight — hold the adjustment screw with a screwdriver so the setting does not change.

Filling the Boiler

Caution — Scalding After choosing Fill boiler the machine starts immediately dispensing hot water. Put a vessel (~1000 ml) underneath the drinks dispenser first. Password level 250 required.
Main switch ON→ RESET→ 9 Password→ enter password→ 2 Machine→ 4 Programs→ 3 Fill boiler→ 7 Reset automat

When restarted, the heater heats water to the programmed temperature (default 97 °C). The boiler is secured by two safety temperature regulators (STR) that switch off the heater if temperature gets too high.

Draining the Boiler (before transport / long shutdown)

Caution — Scalding After selecting Empty Boiler, the machine starts immediately and dispenses hot water. Put a vessel with about 1500 ml capacity underneath the drinks dispensers first. Password level 250 required.
  1. Turn off the machine water supply.
  2. Open the machine door.
  3. Press RESET → Operator Menu.
  4. Press 9 (Password) → key in password.
  5. Press 2 (Machine).
  6. Press 3 (Empty Boiler).
  7. Boiler is drained. Switch off the machine after about 10 seconds.

Calibration

Important — Wrong dosage Before each calibration, a 3× test-dosage must be performed to make sure the dispenser coils are filled. Calibration must be performed for each container. When changing a container, motor or product, always calibrate again.

Access: Operator Menu → 9 Password → password → 2 Machine → 1 Components → select container with ◁ ▷ → 4 Calibr. [gr/kEMK].

  1. Demount the component underneath the container (brewing unit or mixer) and disassemble it.
  2. Have a vessel ready to catch the ingredient and a scale (0.1 g resolution) — zero it with the empty vessel.
  3. Press START twice. After 3 seconds there is a threefold dispensation.
  4. Weigh the served ingredient.
  5. Compare with the amount stated by the machine.
  6. Press RESET and key in the calculated amount.
  7. Save with START.
  8. Test dosage is requested → start with START → after 3 s the ingredient is served → weigh and compare.
  9. If amounts do not match, calibrate again. Otherwise leave with repeated RESET.
Tip Write the calibration numbers down. If you must calibrate again (e.g. after replacing a Main- or I/O-Board), you can key in these numbers directly.

Recipe Settings

Password level 100 required.

Open door → RESET → 9 Password → password → 2 Machine → 3 Recipes → choose product group → choose product → choose ingredient → choose dosage level → change with ◁ ▷ or key in numbers → save with START → repeated RESET returns to main menu.

Note An ingredient in square brackets [ ] means the setting is inherited from a superior product and can only be changed there.

Final Control

  • Check the machine is waterproof — special attention to couplings, connections and links.
  • Control dispensed water amounts and product doses.
  • Lock the front door and pull out the key.

Daily Operation

Product selection, replenishment and consumables.

Product Selection — Step by Step

  1. Place a cup (or pot) underneath the product dispensation units. The display shows Please select.
  2. For a pot: push the cup table to the right first.
  3. Select a product using the number keys 0–9.
  4. Select the strength (e.g. coffee amount) with the arrow keys ◁ and ▷.
  5. Confirm with START. The drink is served.
  6. The display shows Product preparation.
  7. Only remove your cup or pot when the display shows Remove product.
  8. For a pot: push the cup table back into starting position.
Note Before setting the strength with the arrow keys, the additional product must be reactivated with the selection keys. Non-illuminated keys are non-functional.

Product Replenishment

  1. Open the machine door.
  2. Slightly lift the product container at the front (1) and pull it out towards you (2).
  3. Lift the lid off the container you want to replenish and put in the product.
  4. Repeat for every product — except whole coffee beans (BTC / ES).
  5. For whole beans (BTC / ES): lift off the lid of the separate bean container and put in the beans.
Important Products may only be filled in loosely. Avoid any compression.
Note After first filling — or after filling from completely empty (cleaning) — run a couple of test dispensations so the dispenser coils fill up completely. Only use suitable products and note their use-by date.

Cup Dispenser Replenishment

  • Capacity: up to 1000 cups — 500 per dispensing side.
  • Open the machine door.
  • Fill each of the ten shaft tubes (five per dispensing side) from the top with up to 100 cups.
  • Adjust the cup rings so that roughly every 25 cm there is one cup ring in place (they are at the very top at delivery).
Attention Max cup height 13 cm, max pot height 25 cm. If one side runs out, drinks can only be dispensed into separate cups on that side.

Stick Dispenser (version ES)

  • SL 1000 ES can add a stick automatically — left dispensing side only.
  • Sticks must be between 96 and 116 mm long.
  1. Open the machine door.
  2. Remove the weight axis from the stick dispenser at the back of the machine door.
  3. Take the bunch of sticks from the packaging and lay them out on a flat surface as accurately as possible.
  4. From the top, push the bunch of sticks into the stick feeder.
  5. Remove the seal from the bunch of sticks.
  6. Fill the stick feeder to about 10 mm below the upper rim.
  7. Put the weight axis back in.
  8. Close the machine door.

Sugar Container (version ES)

  1. Take off the lid of the sugar container.
  2. Replenish the sugar.
  3. Close the lid again.

Test & Service Menu — Which Button to Press

Complete key sequences for every service task.

Keyboard Test (what button to press)

Purpose Verifies that every key on the front panel is recognised by the mainboard. Use this before replacing a keyboard or mainboard.
  1. Open the machine door.
  2. Press RESET → you reach the Operator Menu.
  3. Press 9 (Enter password).
  4. Key in your password using the number keys, confirm with START.
  5. Press 4 (Test functions).
  6. Press 1 (Keyboard test).
  7. Press each key one at a time. The display must react / show the identifier of the key you pressed.
  8. Any key that produces no reaction is faulty — check the keypad ribbon cable and its connector on the mainboard (75360, located in the machine door).
  9. If all keys are dead, look for fault code KBH01 (Keyboard does not respond).
  10. Exit with repeated RESET back to the main menu.

Related: 4 (Test functions) → 2 (LCD test) checks the display itself.

Replacing the Boiler (as required)

Caution — Machine parts under pressure Danger of injury and damage by components under (hot) water pressure.

Remove Boiler

  1. Put a vessel underneath to catch the boiler water.
  2. In the Operator menu, start the programme Empty Boiler.
  3. Interrupt the water supply.
  4. Allow the machine to cool off.
  5. Remove the cover plate of the middle block.
  6. Loosen the boiler connections.
  7. Remove the old boiler.

Install Boiler

  1. Put in the new boiler.
  2. Connect the boiler.
  3. Reinstall the cover plate.
  4. Switch on the machine.
  5. Re-establish the water supply.
  6. In the Operator menu, choose Fill boiler.
  7. Restart the machine.

Water circuit components: safety valve 15 bar · outlet block (FB / IN / HW) · temperature sensor · boiler and couplings · tubes · water pump · airpump · flow-meter · inlet valve · display.

Module 02

Safety Precautions

Read all warnings and cautions carefully before operating, servicing, or installing the machine.

⚠️ WARNING — Risk of Death or Serious Injury

  • Installation must be by a professional installer or your dealer.
  • Comply with Road Act, Fire Service Act, Food Sanitation Act, Electricity Business Act.
  • Use a single-phase 100V outlet rated 15A+ independently.
  • Never use extension cords or branched outlets — risk of fire.
  • Do not connect ground to gas pipe, water pipe, telephone ground, or lightning rod.
  • Install earth leakage breaker where mandated.
  • Install on strong, level floor. Avoid dust and corrosive gas.
  • Always wear protective equipment (safety shoes, non-slip gloves).
  • Never climb on top of the machine.
  • Always connect ground — incomplete ground risks electric shock.
  • Take earthquake tipping-prevention measures.
  • Do not store ether, benzene, alcohol, LP gas, or chemicals inside.
  • Always turn off power before replacing lamps or cleaning interior.

⚠️ CAUTION — Risk of Injury or Property Damage

  • Do not touch moving parts (cup transport, impeller, brewer, mill, ice maker).
  • Be careful of your head after crouched work.
  • Confirm hoses are inserted after cleaning — hot water may spurt.
  • Apply rinse hot water only to drain pan and bend stage.
  • Return rinse valve to specified position after use.
  • Perform daily hygiene per Food Sanitation Act.

Startup Checklist — Try it!

  • Turn earth leakage breaker ON — interior lamp lights, service outlet powered

  • Set clock: Timer → 0 0 → enter date → Set
  • Release food-sanitation hold: press Food Sanitation Reset

Module 08

Cleaning Procedures

Interactive checklist — tick off items as you complete them. Progress is saved locally.

① Every Visit

  • Run Service → Sanitation (brewer + impeller + water passage)
  • Rinse ice chute + ice hopper with rinse valve hot water
  • Clean cup transport Y-axis, cup hand, slatted floor
  • Clean drain pan (resin + SUS surfaces)
  • Wipe cabinet interior, door inner surface
  • Wipe bend stage + auto door inside
  • Empty + wash drain bucket; place float back inside
  • Empty waste bucket + clean machine floor
  • Collect trash; clean vending machine surroundings

③ Every 1000 Bends or Monthly

  • Clean ventilation filter + intake filter (2 locations)
  • Wipe canister lid + body with damp towel
  • Remove mill adjustment dial, brush internal ingredients
  • Remove + wash retention chute; wipe dry before reinstall
  • Remove + wash RC chute + mill chute; wipe dry
  • Remove + wash RC brewer (chamber, rubber well, drum, screen seal)
  • Clean ice maker discharge port + ice chute with rinse valve + brush
  • Run Service → Impeller Rinse; wipe impeller + shaft
  • Brush beverage hoses + nozzles; run test rinse
  • Clean condenser filter + brewer filter

④ Every 2–3 Months

  • Clean cup storage inside (alcohol towel)
  • Clean coin mech verification + gate with dedicated towel
  • Clean bill validator belt + rollers (no oil!)
  • Clean machine interior ceiling BEFORE other cleaning
  • Drain + wash water reservoir; wipe float
